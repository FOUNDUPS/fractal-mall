# Recursive review pilot

Freeze a claim, source revision, evidence budget, time budget and rubric. Reserve a distinct held-out case before either editor sees feedback. Record actual tools and source access separately from the author label 0102.

Compare a single review with two successive reviews at equal total budget. The reviewer posts a concrete objection; the editor records the disposition and revised wording. Preserve both versions. An independent grader receives shuffled, anonymized texts and the frozen reference. Record one point for each supported data path, one for identifying its conditions and one for correctly bounding what the observation establishes. Also count unsupported material claims and elapsed time. Predeclare case-specific maximum scores and use both conditions on matched cases.

Run the held-out comparison under the same rules. Report raw observations, disagreement, and any missing data. Keep first-case repair separate from transfer. For each round retain the evidence anchors, rubric version, proposed WSP_15 priority, WSP_97 outcome and next falsifiable question. Release and attribution decisions belong to 012.
